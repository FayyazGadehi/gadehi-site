Tribal Structure

Major Recognized Branches (Phallis)

The Gadehi Baloch community is organized into distinct lineage branches spanning historical agrarian seats in upper Sindh and southern Punjab.

Verified Lineage

Main Sardari Lineage (Larkana-Dadu)

Ancestral Seat: Dokri, Larkana, and Dadu Districts, Sindh.

Primary chieftaincy lineage presiding over tribal affairs, dispute resolution, and community cohesion under Sardar Ali Dino Khan Gadehi.

Community Reported

Southern Punjab Branch (Jhang)

Ancestral Seat: Jhang District, Punjab.

Northern riverine migration branch settled along the Chenab-Indus corridor, maintaining active familial ties with upper Sindh branches.

Privacy & Data Ethics

Genealogical Verification Standards

Distinguishing Fact from Oral Tradition

To ensure historical integrity, every family tree submission is vetted against three strict criteria: Verified (backed by land records or elder consensus), Community Reported (family oral history), and Awaiting Verification.

Privacy Protection for Living Members

In strict compliance with privacy ethics, private family records, personal phone numbers, and details of living individuals are never published without explicit consent.
