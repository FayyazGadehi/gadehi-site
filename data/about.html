About Gadehi Baloch Global Heritage

A long-term international digital archive dedicated to preserving our history, ancestors, families, culture, and professional excellence for future generations.

Portal Founder & Architect

Fayyaz Ali Gadehi

Fayyaz Ali Gadehi is a web developer, educator, and digital archivist based in Karachi, Pakistan. Recognizing that scattered oral histories and unrecorded family lineages risk fading across generations, he founded Gadehi Baloch Global Heritage in 2026.

His vision is to build a credible, academically disciplined platform that honors tribal leadership under Sardar Ali Dino Khan Gadehi while uniting Gadehi scholars, doctors, engineers, jurists, and youth across Pakistan and the international diaspora.

Our Three-Tier Verification Charter

VERIFIED
Supported by historical books, colonial gazetteers, official institutional records, or direct elder consensus.

COMMUNITY REPORTED
Submitted by verified community members or family elders, preserved with clear attribution while awaiting archival cross-check.

AWAITING VERIFICATION
Held in private editorial review until lineage connection and factual accuracy are confirmed.
