Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Who founded and manages this portal?

The platform was founded and is owned by Fayyaz Ali Gadehi as an international non-commercial historical and community archive.

Are "Gadehi" and "Gadhi" the same tribe?

Both spellings appear in historical revenue records and modern identification documents for Gadehi Baloch families. However, because similar phonetic surnames exist among unrelated groups, our archive verifies Baloch lineage and ancestral settlement before listing any family.

How long does profile verification take?

Submissions made via the Contribution Hub are reviewed by our editorial desk and regional elders, typically within 3 to 7 days.
