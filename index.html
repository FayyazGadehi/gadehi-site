Biography & Tribal Leadership

Sardar Ali Dino Khan Gadehi (Gadhi) is the esteemed Tribal Chief (Sardar) of the Gadehi Baloch community. Based in Taluka Dokri, District Larkana, he serves as the central pillar of unity, traditional justice, and cultural preservation for Gadehi families across Sindh and Punjab.

Alongside his tribal responsibilities, he has dedicated decades to public service and local governance. He has served as the General Secretary of the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) for Taluka Dokri and has been elected twice as a Member of the District Council Larkana.

Chieftaincy of the Gadehi Tribe

Leading the Gadehi (Gadhi) Baloch tribe with dignity, resolving community matters, and strengthening brotherhood across Larkana, Dadu, Sukkur, and Jhang.

Twice Elected District Council Member

Elected twice by public mandate to represent his constituency in District Council Larkana.
