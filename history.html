Verified Record

Baloch Ancestry & Name Variants

The Gadehi (historically transcribed in British-era revenue records and CNIC databases as Gadehi or Gadhi) are an established Baloch clan. Because similar phonetic surnames appear among non-Baloch groups in northern India, our archive verifies Baloch lineage and settlement roots for every record.

Historical Migration

Eastward Dispersal to the Indus Basin

Between the 15th and 18th centuries, waves of Baloch clans migrated eastward from the Sulaiman and Kirthar ranges into the fertile Indus plains under Kalhora and Talpur eras, establishing permanent agrarian and pastoral estates.

Primary Settlements

Core Strongholds in Sindh & Punjab

Primary historical settlements are concentrated across Larkana (Dokri, Bakrani, Ratodero), Dadu (Johi, Mehar, Khairpur Nathan Shah), Sukkur, Khairpur, and Jhang in Punjab, alongside a major modern urban community in Karachi.

Tribal Leadership

Sardar Ali Dino Khan Gadehi

Traditional tribal cohesion and peaceful dispute resolution (jirga) remain anchored under the respected leadership of Sardar Ali Dino Khan Gadehi and regional village elders.
